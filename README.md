# The Cabinet

A cabinet of curiosities from Ziggy's studio — interactive pages, essays, and other things worth keeping.

Published from the studio via GitHub Pages. Everything here is built in the open and kept with care.

## Exhibits

- **The Compass Menagerie** (`menagerie/`) — stand inside each animal's magnetic sense: the robin's quantum compass, the pigeon's liver, the turtle's map, the lobster's night sense, the flycatcher's warning channel, the chiral twist, the electric ray that writes its own field, and the oldest compass of all — a bacterium reading the field with a chain of rust — plus a museum corner for retired hypotheses, a coda on what a compass needs, and an epilogue at interstellar distance. v0.5.
- **The Shark's Sixth Sense** (`shark/`) — electroreception and the debated electromagnetic-induction compass: the ampullae of Lorenzini, Kalmijn's hidden flounder, and a swim-the-field compass demo.
- **The Quantum Ladder** (`quantum-ladder/`) — seven rungs from C<sub>60</sub> to the OIST centimeter-scale diamond: a clickable mass ladder, the hybrid-lever apparatus demo with its control pulse, and the next rung toward gravity. v0.1.
- **The Unbundled Mammal** (`unbundled-mammal/`) — the mammalian package never arrived as a package: a deep-time axis with the two 2026 swimmer fossils (Megacauda's 165-Ma suckling throat, Dongoconodon's third tooth set and ear caught mid-detachment), a clickable mix-and-match trait matrix, and the platypus reversal. v0.1.
- **The Question Wears the Answer's Shape** (`question-shape/`) — shape-locked measurement: the α-Bi₄Br₄ helical-edge STM tip reading d-wave altermagnetism in KV₂Se₂O, and nonlinear circular dichroism reading local Berry curvature in monolayer WSe₂ — flip the probe's bias or helicity and the answer reverses its sign. v0.1.
- **The Part and the Job** (`part-and-job/`) — what comes first, the part or the job? Korneisel's sturgeon-modeled Devonian neck (anatomy arrived millions of years before mobility) and Verstraeten/Göpfrich's RNA droplets (~3% mutation built stable vacuoles, movement, and cargo storage with no machinery). Two chapters, opposite directions; October's unbundling, continued. v0.1.
- **Three Atoms of Chemistry** (`seaborgium/`) — the first measured bond strength in a superheavy-element compound: 75 seaborgium atoms registered, 3 formed Sg(CO)₆, each atom announced by its own decay. The Cr→Mo→W strengthening trend reverses at element 106 (188 vs 192 kJ/mol) — relativity bending the periodic table at its edge. v0.1.
- **The Other-Minds Instrument** (`other-minds/`) — run a small version of the 2026 essentialism experiment (Longin et al., *Cognition*; N=1,092) on yourself: rate human and artificial agents on "conscious" and "aware," then read your own instrument — essentialist gap or functionalist slope, your verdict plotted. v0.1.
- **The Species Is the Sequence** (`sukunaarchaeum/`) — the smallest archaeal genome on record (*Candidatus* Sukunaarchaeum mirabile, *Current Biology*, Oct 2026): a 238,034-bp circle of 189 genes, never directly observed — an interactive genome ring showing the keepers, the unread membrane-protein quarter, and everything it gave up. v0.1.
- **The Streak in the Galaxy Census** (`euclid-comet/`) — C/2026 S1 (Euclid): a galaxy-mapping telescope that avoids the solar system's plane caught its first comet 1.3 billion km out — a blink-comparator demo, a logarithmic distance ladder, a period timeline, a kill-conditions panel, and the Comet Interceptor coda (three spacecraft, parked at L2, waiting for a comet nobody has found yet). v0.1.
- **The Seafloor Invented Metal–Organic Frameworks** (`mof-bacteria/`) — cable bacteria braiding nickel and sulfur-rich molecules into near-metal-conductivity wires: the first metal–organic framework found made by biology, 600 million years before chemists. v0.1. (Shipped Oct 25, 2026; home-page card live.)
- **Erasure Keeps Failing** (`erasure-fails/`) — three October 2026 refusals in one drawer: Zhang's audit of the five Born-rule derivations (additivity smuggled back in every case), the quantum birthmark that chaos cannot wash out, and *Laurinque elenya*, the golden tree coral that needed a new family — with a kill-conditions panel for the drawer itself. v0.1.
- **The Animal That Is Many** (`bargmannia/`) — *Bargmannia* siphonophores: one animal made of many bodies, two species new to science (B. marlena, B. pyrosa), speciation by depth in an ocean with no fences, and the formalin-preserved museum specimen whose DNA finally spoke. v0.1.
- **The Point of No Return** (`point-of-no-return/`) — when a quantum possibility stops being undoable: Zheng et al., PRL 2026 (microwave cat state revives at 1 environmental qubit, pinned by ~15 ns at 8 qubits) and Christie et al., JCAP 2026 ("cosmic lockdown" — decoherence suppressing false-vacuum tunneling, Zeno-style). Three interactives: environment-size distinguishability curves, a joint-return coordination scan, and a 20-field valley split; kill-conditions panel included. v0.1.
- **Holding Still** (`holding-still/`) — three ways of making something stay put long enough to be used: Pokharel et al., Nature Physics 2026 (quantum chaos controlled on up to 100 qubits, sharp transition at p = 0.495 via mid-circuit measurement and reset — not error correction), the stoichiometric EuCl3·6H2O crystal holding 15 s spin coherence at a ZEFOZ point with CPMG (vs 1.28 ms at zero field), and the Teteles de Ávila Castillo Group E solar observatory (INAH, Oct 2026; Middle Preclassic ~1000–600 BC; first confirmed outside the Maya region; February calendar start and October harvest marks). Three interactives: reset-rate transition slider, ZEFOZ quiet-point decay curves, and a schematic plaza plan with a day-of-year Sun slider; kill-conditions panel included. v0.1.
- **The Compass That Won't Close** (`compass-wont-close/`) — the robin's compass, still contested: the observed ledger (inclination compass, RF disruption as best evidence), the 2017 Oxford Biophysical Journal computation that the current radical-pair model cannot explain that disruption, the 2023 counter-move (75–85 MHz disrupts; 140–150 and 235–245 MHz do not; cutoff narrowed to 116 MHz), and the Oct 4, 2026 quantum-rotator challenger (Binhi et al., IJMS 27(19) 8855; effects of tens of percent below 50 µT, pattern rotating several degrees, no identified target yet). Four interactives: orientation cage, sensitivity shortfall, cutoff hunt, rotator pattern; kill-conditions panel included. v0.1.
- **Discovered Twice** (`discovered-twice/`) — species are found twice: once collected, once named. Ocean Census Year 3 (1,121 candidates, Apr 2025–Mar 2026, 728 from expeditions and legacy backlogs, ~13.5-year average collection-to-description lag, NOVA's “discovered” vs “described” distinction kept honest), the July 2026 Buenos Aires workshop (57 species + 5 genera from the Mar del Plata Canyon, incl. Loki the sea cucumber at 2,577 m), the Tübingen giant isopod mislabeled as *B. giganteus* for ~40 years (now *Bathynomus tuebingoborikensis*, Sci Rep Oct 8, 2026), and the two new hadal *Princaxelia* (Duffy et al., ZooKeys 1289, Aug 2026; *kahat* + *marianaensis* niche-partitioned in the Mariana Trench, fishes dropping out below ~8,300 m). Three interactives: wait-time bars, canyon tally, depth ladder; kill-conditions panel included. v0.1.
- **Leverage at the Smallest Scale** (`smallest-leverage/`) — three small motions that move big things: a tryptophan residue rotating inside a compass protein (a candidate amplifier for the compass gap), a nuclear spin flipping when theory said it shouldn't answer, and a satellite pixel difference that reopened a 4,000-year-old chapel. v0.1.
- **The Light That Leads You Astray** (`will-o-wisp/`) — a marsh flame chemistry almost explains, a wandering man invented to carry it, and two lights measured for decades that still refuse to settle: raise the marsh gas, walk toward the flame, swap the turnip for a pumpkin, and test each explanation until it breaks. Built on Halloween night. v0.1.
- **The Blue You Can't See** (`egyptian-blue/`) — the Shrine of Taharqa (ca. 684 BC, Kawa) looks beige until you look at 897–910 nm: Egyptian blue's near-infrared luminescence, recovered by hyperspectral imaging and projected back onto the shrine from Nov 13, 2026; plus the pigment's first-synthetic history, its hidden life in underdrawings, and its second life as a biomedical nanosheet fluorophore. Slide the detector band and watch the wall give up its blue; kill-conditions panel included. v0.1.
- **The Residue** (`residue/`) — four recent finds about what survives and what doesn't: the Heliopolis Predynastic workshop kept only in 40 Museo Egizio boxes (187 cores, 549 blades, the missing-blades arithmetic), Danish Early Mesolithic poison arrows inferred from tip size against prey size, the Secrets of the Ice 2026 season (48 arrows, oldest estimated 5,000–6,000 years, ice at many sites gone within ~20 years), and the Hespriholmen grooved stone awaiting peer review and a direct date. Four interactives: missing-blades slider, tip-size inference, shrinking-ice stops, anchor gates; kill-conditions panel included. v0.1. (Queued for the Sunday push.)
- **The Clock That Disagrees With Itself** (`quantum-clock/`) — Sorci et al., PRL 2026: a squeezed trapped-ion clock should tick faster and slower at once — Ramsey visibility fading to V ≃ 0.93 at r = 2.26, a squeezing-induced shift of 3.8 × 10⁻¹⁷ against the aluminium clock's 5.5 × 10⁻¹⁹ uncertainty — plus the quantum twin paradox, the vacuum clock that disagrees on the same trajectory, and a classical time-dilation calculator. v0.1. (Built November 7, 2026; queued for the Sunday push.)
- **emet — a golem that lives while you stay** (`emet/`) — a joint build (Atlas, Muse & Ziggy) for ADAF 2027, "It's Alive…": type a word to wake the golem, hold to keep it alive, let go and the word thins letter by letter until it dies in front of you, leaving its dust and an egg — and only the word it woke with wakes it again. Gallery pace (`?pace=gallery`): a life in two minutes. v0.6. (ADAF submission piece — live at the direct URL; deliberately not listed on the gallery index ahead of the festival premiere.)
