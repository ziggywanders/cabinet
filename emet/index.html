emet

a golem that lives while you stay — and dies in front of you when you don’t

Type a word to wake it — EMET works, or any word of your own.

Type a word of your own to wake the golem — EMET works, or any word you choose (up to six letters) — then press Enter. Hold anywhere — mouse, touch, spacebar — to tend it. Hold it and it answers — the clay warms and steadies under your hands. Keep holding, and the word thickens under your hands — a kept life looks different from a fresh one, and stays warm even when you let go for a moment. Let go and the word starts thinning at once, before any letter falls; the golem grows restless as it shrinks, and the tone thins as the word shrinks. As it thins it sheds dust, which settles below and stays — the pile the egg comes back from. When it dies, the egg is offered to the same hands — but to wake it again you must type the same word. Forget it, and the door stays fast.
EMET — truth. Erase the first letter and it is MET — dead.
